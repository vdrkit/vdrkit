## Why we ask for this

VDRkit is released under the GNU Affero General Public License version 3 (AGPL-3.0). The project owner also wants to
be able to offer VDRkit under other terms, for example a commercial licence for organisations that cannot use the
AGPL, or a hosted service. To do that, the project owner needs your permission for your contribution. This agreement
gives that permission. You keep the copyright in your contribution, and your contribution always stays available
under the AGPL-3.0.

The project owner and copyright holder is Shibula (Pty) Ltd, which can be reached at cla@vdrkit.com.
This agreement is for contributors who sign as individuals. There is no agreement for contributions made on behalf of
a company yet: if you want to contribute on behalf of a company, open an issue first and we will add one.

## How to sign

You sign this agreement by posting the comment that the CLA Assistant bot asks for on your first pull request:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

The bot records your GitHub user name, the date and the pull request. One signature covers all your later
contributions to VDRkit under this version of the agreement. If you contribute as part of your job, see section 3
before you sign.
